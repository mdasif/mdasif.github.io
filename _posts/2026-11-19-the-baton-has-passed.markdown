<p>
Today is like any other day but not for me, of course.
</p>
<p>
Today's the day when the burdens of all the what-ifs of my life become unbearable. What if I had chosen to never move away from home? what if I had returned home? what if I had made that trip earlier? What if I had called home more often? Today's the day I work up a year full of courage to face. A day which I devote to despair, for I leave no room in my life for it otherwise. To lay myself bare to all the demons of my mind. And survive.
</p>
<p>
This year, the day has come — and it feels different. The sadness is there, the way it always is. But something in me has changed in this last year, and the change has a name: fatherhood.
</p>
<p>
The baton has passed. not quite yet and I haven't let go.
</p>
<p>
I remember my father this day, as I do every year, with gratitude. Only this time, remembering him does not only take me backwards. This year, this week also looks forward.
</p>
